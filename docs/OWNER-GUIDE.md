# Your shop: a simple guide for the owner

You run everything from the **admin panel**: your website address plus `/admin` (for example
`https://yourshop.com/admin`). Sign in with the email and password you were given. Nobody can
find the admin from the shop itself.

Every screen has a short note at the top that says what it is for. If something is not clear,
that is a mistake in the screen, so please tell us.

## Your day, step by step

1. Open **Dashboard**. It shows today's, this week's and this month's sales, and a **to-do list**:
   paid orders to send, orders that need you, reviews waiting, new enquiries.
2. Open **Orders**. The tab **To send** lists paid orders that are waiting for you.
3. Pack the order, book the courier, then open the order and press **Mark as shipped**. Type the
   tracking number (and the tracking link if you have one). The customer is emailed automatically.
4. When it arrives, press **Mark as delivered**.
5. Answer new **Enquiries** (people who filled in the "find your outfit" form).
6. Open **Reviews**. Press **Show on website** for good ones, **Hide** for others, and **Reply** to anyone.

## Orders

- **Needs attention** (a tab and a red note on the order) means something odd happened, for example a
  customer was charged twice or paid after the stock ran out. Read the note, fix it, then press
  "I have dealt with this".
- **Refund money**: choose the whole amount or part of it, and write a reason. The money goes back to
  the customer's card or PayPal.
- **Cancel and refund**: for a paid order that you cannot fulfil. The stock goes back on the shelf.
- **Waiting for payment**: the customer started to pay but did not finish. After 30 minutes the order
  cancels itself and the stock is released. You do not need to do anything.
- The **private note** on an order is only for you. Customers never see it.

## Products

- **Products → Add a product.** Give it a name, price, photos and a category.
- Use the **size and colour generator**: tick the sizes and colours and the rows are made for you. Then
  type how many you have of each.
- **Price shown to customers** is the real price. Add an **original price** only if you want a crossed-out
  "sale" price.
- A product with orders cannot be deleted (your order history must stay correct). Use **Archive** instead.
- **Collections** group products for shoppers (Eid Edit, Mehndi Picks). A *manual* collection is a list you
  pick. A *smart* collection fills itself by rules, for example "everything tagged Eid".
- **Filters** are the choices shoppers can tick, such as Fabric or Occasion. You can add new ones.

## Prices, shipping and tax

- All prices you type are in **Australian dollars and include GST**.
- **Currencies**: choose which currencies customers can switch to and the exchange rate. Customers see
  prices in their currency; your reports stay in AUD.
- **Shipping**: make a *zone* (a list of countries) and add shipping options with a price and an optional
  "free over" amount. One zone must be the "rest of the world".
- **Settings → GST**: your GST rate, and the switch **"Orders sent overseas are GST-free"**.
- **Coupons**: create a code like EID10 (percentage off, money off, or free shipping), with an optional
  minimum order, a limit on uses, and dates. Switch it off to pause it. A code that was used on orders
  cannot be deleted, only switched off.

## The website's words and pictures

- **Pages** (About, Shipping, Returns, Contact) and the **homepage** are built from blocks. Drag to reorder.
- **Menus**: the top menu and the footer links.
- **Banners**: the big pictures. You can set start and end dates.
- **FAQ**: the questions and answers. They also help Google show your answers.
- **Redirects**: when you rename a product or page, the old link is forwarded automatically. You can add
  your own here too.

## Settings

- **Store details, WhatsApp and social links** appear in the footer and on the contact page.
- **Emails to customers**: switch the order confirmation and shipping emails on or off, and add a line
  (like your opening hours) at the bottom of every email.
- **Customer reviews**: leave "show new reviews straight away" **off** so you read each one first.
- **Visitor tracking**: paste your Meta Pixel ID and Google Analytics ID if you use them.

## The QR code for your shop window

Open **Enquiries** and press **Copy QR code link**. Paste that link into any free QR code maker, print the
code, and put it in your window. People who scan it fill in a short form and appear in Enquiries.

## Facebook and Instagram shop

Ask your developer for your **product feed address** (it ends in `/api/feeds/meta.csv`). In Meta Commerce
Manager choose to add products from a data feed and paste that address. Your products and stock then stay
up to date there by themselves.

## Good to know

- After you change something, the website updates within a few seconds. Very occasionally the first
  visitor still sees the old version for a moment.
- The **Customers** screen lets you switch a customer off. They are signed out at once and cannot sign
  in again until you switch them back on. Their orders are kept.
- Never share your admin password. If you think someone else knows it, tell your developer to change it.
